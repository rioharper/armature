# ADR 0006 — Actuators and the bus are sized from a mission, by one engine module

2026-10-12 — Status: accepted (replaces `design-foundations.md:56`'s "sizing calculator or spreadsheet" and the static holding torque as the sizing case)

Every actuator and DC-bus claim a project makes is computed by `armature.drive`,
one module of the engine (ADR 0004), from a **mission**: named segments run in
order, with a duty cycle. A **move** goes from a start to an end, or from a start
along a direction for a distance, under velocity, acceleration and jerk limits,
generated by ruckig. A **hold** keeps a posture for a duration under a load. An
**event** starts from a state, or partway through a segment, and is
forward-simulated with each motor coasting, shorted, flux-braking or following a
torque profile. Moves and holds become torque through `State.inverse`, sample by
sample. Events run on a copy of the compiled model, with the passive modes as
joint damping under `implicitfast`. The electrical model is our own and
quasi-static, using the conventions of Lee et al. (arXiv:2310.00080):
- τ = Kt·i and V = R_φ·i + Ke·ω, with a current limit and a voltage limit set by the bus;
- copper loss with R(T) and a single-node thermal RC;
- gearbox efficiency as a forward and a backward band, and a constant driver efficiency.

Every electrical constant names its basis. Kt is cross-checked against Ke, and
against a published torque–speed curve where one exists. One shared bus carries:
- a source, a voltage band that can or cannot sink;
- a capacitance band;
- a brake resistor;
- flux braking, whose heat enters the motor's winding;
- the pack;
- a constant hotel load.

The module generates the margins:
- the torque–speed envelope at the bus's lowest voltage;
- winding temperature over the duty, or RMS torque against the continuous rating where no thermal RC is published;
- driver peak and RMS current;
- bus peak and minimum voltage against the trips on that bus;
- the brake resistor's rating;
- the source's power.

Each is searched by `armature.search` (ADR 0005). A margin whose data is missing
reports UNCHECKED and fails unless the project argues an exemption.

None of Plumb, Ibex or CineWave V2 sized over a trajectory. Every sizing is a
peak or steady named case, worked by hand in spec §9. Gearbox efficiency is a
guess or absent, and bus capacitance is unknown in all three. Regen reached all
three:
- Plumb's `max_voltage` fault cascade (spec rev 1.1, F2, Blocker);
- its regen budget read on the symmetric descent, 49 W, when one cable paying out returns 128 W (F5);
- Ibex's body switch removing the only sink, 6.6 J against 0.26 J of storage;
- CineWave v1's blown moteus, regenerating into a PSU that cannot sink.

Both wrong torque constants found so far were basis errors: Plumb's 9.55/Kv
against its datasheet's 8.27/Kv, and Ibex's secant through saturation. Probed on
2026-10-12, `inverse` along a 1 s move costs 0.2 s at 1 kHz and matches
`mj_inverse` to 9e-16 on an open chain. A 25-line bus model put a 2.4 s geared
swing at 222 V on 220 µF with no brake, and 30.2 V with an 8 Ω brake.

Rejected:
- **MuJoCo's `dcmotor`.** Its supply sources and sinks without limit, so regen vanishes. It folds efficiency into K symmetrically, which is wrong for back-driving. Its multi-input controller sets `model.nu = 3` for one actuator, which the engine reads as the actuator count. Its controller broke compatibility in 3.12. Its current state is unclamped: an undersized probe read 70 A and a 593 K rise.
- **Forward simulation with a tracking controller for commanded moves.** Controller tuning becomes a sizing input: Kp = 400 went unstable on the engine's 1 ms Euler step.
- **Load cases as acceleration sets.** They give no RMS, temperature or energy.
- **Sizing per project.** It has already forked: Ibex's `regen.py:62` hard-codes R_phase and Kt past its own parameters.
- **pint to catch basis errors.** A_rms and A_peak share a dimension.
- **A fixed headroom factor for control effort.** It is an invented number.

The cost accepted: inverse dynamics assumes perfect tracking, so controller
effort, overshoot and disturbance rejection are absent; a project adds a
disturbance as a hold load or a band. Inductance and iron loss are left out
(L/R ≈ 0.9 ms for RobStride 02). There is one bus per model, and no
state-of-charge model. Revisit:
- if a bench measurement disagrees with a margin beyond its band (Plumb's T3.8 brake pulse, a measured thermal time constant);
- if a project needs two buses;
- if closed-loop torque matters enough to need a controls overhaul.
